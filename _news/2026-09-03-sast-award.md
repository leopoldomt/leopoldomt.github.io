*Evaluating Shaker for Flaky Test Detection in Python Projects*, co-authored with [Gabriela Leal (CIn-UFPE)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gabrielamfleal/) and [Denini Silva (UFRPE)](https://denini08.github.io/), has been selected as a Distinguished Paper at the 11th Brazilian Symposium on Systematic and Automated Software Testing (SAST 2026)! Not only that, but it also has received the Distinguished Artifact Award at the CBSoft 2026 Artifact Festival. 

Congratulations to Gabriela and Denini for an outstanding job! See you at CBSoft 2026!
